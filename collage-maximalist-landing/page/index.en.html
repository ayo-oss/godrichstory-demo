Decide three things and it turns into collage

The angle list, the text-safe colors, the shadow weight. Lock those three and everything else can stack as freely as you like.

01

Four angles, no more

-3°, 2°, -1.5°, 5°. Stray outside this list and it stops reading as collage and starts reading as an alignment mistake.

02

Colors that carry text

White text only on coral, sky, and grape. Lemon, lime, and blush are decoration-only, so they take ink text alone.

03

Shadow means thickness

5px at blur 0 when stuck down, 16px when lifted. Mix in one soft shadow and the cut-and-paste feel is gone.
